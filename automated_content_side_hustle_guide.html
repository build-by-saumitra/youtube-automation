A practical 12-month roadmap treating this as a side hustle alongside your primary work.

M1–2
Pick your niche and set up
Choose 1 channel (YouTube first — higher earning ceiling). Pick a niche: AI tools, finance, or data careers. Create accounts, set up CapCut + ElevenLabs + VidIQ. Goal: publish your first 5 videos.
M3–5
Consistent output — 2 videos/week
Test titles and thumbnails (they decide 80% of clicks). Add affiliate links from day 1. Study retention analytics — find where viewers drop off. Build a 30-video backlog before you expect traction.
M6
Hit YPP threshold → monetize
Target: 1000 subscribers + 4000 watch hours. Apply for YouTube Partner Program. First AdSense cheque usually small — focus on building. Start affiliate income now if not already.
M7–9
Add Instagram as a distribution channel
Repurpose YouTube Shorts clips as Instagram Reels. Use ManyChat for DM automation. Don't start Instagram from scratch — it's a YouTube amplifier at this stage.
M10–12
Scale and diversify income
Launch a digital product (template pack, mini-course, prompt library). Pitch to 2–3 SaaS brands in your niche for sponsorships. Consider a second channel or outsource scripting on Fiverr/Upwork.

Which platform should you start with?

Recommended first
YouTube
Higher income ceiling, content is evergreen (videos earn for years), AdSense is the most reliable Indian creator income, and your IT/AI niche has massive CPM potential.
Add later
Instagram
Good for brand deals and affiliate, but requires continuous posting. Best used as a distribution channel after YouTube is established — repurpose Shorts as Reels.
Time commitment reality: Plan for 8–12 hrs/week in months 1–6. After you've built templates and workflows, it drops to 4–6 hrs/week. Don't quit if traction is slow — 90% of channels that earn serious money took 9–18 months.
